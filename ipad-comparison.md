# iPad Comparison: Current Lineup vs. iPad Air (5th generation)

A size and weight comparison of Apple's current iPad lineup, with the **iPad Air (5th generation)** included as a reference point.

> **Note:** All weights listed are for **Wi‑Fi models**. Cellular (Wi‑Fi + Cellular) versions add roughly 1–3 g. Dimensions are listed as height × width × depth.

## How the Current Models Compare to the iPad Air 5th Gen (461 g, 10.9")

### Weight

| Model | Difference vs. iPad Air (5th gen) |
|---|---|
| iPad mini (A17 Pro) | ~168 g **lighter** (biggest difference) |
| iPad Pro 11" (M5) | ~17 g **lighter** |
| iPad Air 11" (M4) | ~3 g heavier (essentially identical) |
| iPad (A16) | ~16 g heavier |
| iPad Pro 13" (M5) | ~118 g heavier |
| iPad Air 13" (M4) | ~155 g heavier |

### Size

- The **iPad Air 11" (M4)** has a nearly identical footprint to the 5th‑gen Air (same 6.1 mm thickness, within ~0.2 mm on each edge) — but a slightly larger 11" screen in the same body thanks to thinner bezels.
- The **iPad Pro 11" (M5)** is the thinnest of the 11"-class at 5.3 mm, and slightly narrower.
- The **13" models** are in a different size class entirely — noticeably taller, wider, and heavier.
- The **mini** is the most compact in every dimension.

## Takeaways

- **Coming from an iPad Air (5th gen) and want the same feel?** The **iPad Air 11" (M4)** is essentially a drop-in replacement in size and weight, just with a newer chip and a slightly bigger screen.
- **Want it lighter and smaller?** The **mini** is dramatically more portable, and the **Pro 11"** is a touch lighter and thinner.
- **Want a bigger screen?** Both 13" models are a significant jump in size and weight.
